Executive view

Three scales of the same geography

The study connects public safety, victimisation profiles and rural conditions. Results are descriptive and presented alongside their main methodological limitations.

Key findings

Volume and territory

Large urban centres lead absolute counts. Meaningful risk comparisons require population rates and comparable institutional coverage.

Unequal victimisation

Homicide and theft records are predominantly male; sexual offences and domestic violence are predominantly female.

Rural frontiers

Municipalities with the highest illicit-crop shares cluster in southern and western Colombia.

Methodological transparency

What the data can—and cannot—say

Correlation does not imply causation. Records depend on reporting and institutional capacity. The project preserves the historical script, includes a revised R version, and discloses that the original microdata were not supplied.
